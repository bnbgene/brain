Booking policies

PaymentsA $20 non-refundable deposit is required to secure your appointment, sent by Zelle.
Cancellations & no-showsSame-day cancellations and no-call, no-shows are permanently banned from booking. If Dri cancels, your deposit is returned. If you cancel, the deposit isn't refunded.
Grace period & late feeYou get a 15-minute grace period. After that, the appointment is cancelled, or you can agree to a $15 late fee to continue.
Squeeze-in feeHolidays, after-hours and last-minute squeeze-ins add $40 to the service price.

Appointment prep

  • Arrive with your hair freshly washed and blown out.
  • Hair should be free of tangles, oils and other products.
  • All hair must be at least 4 inches long all around.
  • Freshly cut hair and blunt cuts are not recommended.
Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How long will my hair last?

Your braids should last at least 2–3 weeks, and with proper care they'll likely last longer.

How do I take care of my braids?

When you go to sleep, wrap your hair with a silk scarf and bonnet.

Do you prefer a specific type of hair?

Yes. For all extension braided styles, Dri prefers Xpressions braiding hair. Buy the 52-inch pack (3 sections of hair included). Reach out if you need help with how many packs to buy or checking you have the right brand.
