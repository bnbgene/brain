    Call guide

    What to say for every way a call can go. Pick who you're calling and their details fill in.

    Before you dial
    • Open their lead. Know their name, what they do, and one real detail (reviews, years in business).
    • Best times: Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am or 2–4pm their time. Check the time zone when calling another state.
    • Stand up, smile, and talk slower than feels normal. You're a person offering something free, not a telemarketer.
    • Don't record calls. Some states (California included) require everyone's permission.
    • If anyone says don't call again, stop and mark them Not interested.

    Your details

    Used in scripts and email drafts. Email law (CAN-SPAM) requires a real mailing address in every cold email.

    Cold email rules

    • Send 20–30 a day at most from a new inbox.
    • Use your real name and a subject line that matches the email.
    • Include your mailing address and a way to opt out.
    • If someone says stop, mark them "Not interested" and never email again.
    • Read every draft before you send it.
