    Your details

    Used in scripts and email drafts. Email law (CAN-SPAM) requires a real mailing address in every cold email.

    Cold email rules

    • Send 20–30 a day at most from a new inbox.
    • Use your real name and a subject line that matches the email.
    • Include your mailing address and a way to opt out.
    • If someone says stop, mark them "Not interested" and never email again.
    • Read every draft before you send it.
