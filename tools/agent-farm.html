How the farm runs

Scheduled agents

Market Desk, News Desk, Recap Bot and the rest are scheduled Claude tasks, like your morning content brief. They wake up on time, do the work, post, and check in here.

The 24/7 Greeter

A real Discord bot that stays online all the time. It welcomes every new member and answers questions in #ask-gene in your voice. It runs on a small always-on server.

Auto or ask

On Auto-post an agent sends straight to Discord. On Ask me first it parks the post in the queue below for you to approve.

You stay in charge

Talk to any agent in the office to change how it posts, turn it off, or see what it would say.
