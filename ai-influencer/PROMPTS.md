Workflow (from the guide): make an image (Nano Banana Pro or GPT Image 2 in Higgsfield) → check it → animate it (Seedance 2.5, 11s, 720p, 9:16).
Always attach the **master reference image** after Step 1.

Generate 4 at a time, pick the best one, and save it as `kasey_master.png`.

## Step 3: First 10 videos
Each one: **Hook** (on-screen text) → **Image prompt** (add to the base line above) → **Video prompt** (Seedance).
Video base, always prepend: `Handheld iPhone footage, natural slight camera shake, realistic motion and physics, natural lighting, single continuous shot, no cuts, no text, no logos.`

### 1. How I get into my truck (the series opener)
- **Hook:** "y'all keep asking how a 5'0 girl gets in a lifted truck"
- **Image:** `Standing at the driver door of the lifted matte-black truck, the door handle at her shoulder height, looking back at camera with a smirk.`
- **Video:** `She grabs the door frame grab handle, puts one boot on the side step, and hauls herself up into the driver seat with effort, nearly slipping once, then sits and laughs at the camera. Camera held by a friend standing behind her.`

### 2. Gas station stares
- **Hook:** "every. single. time."
- **Image:** `At a rural gas station pump, standing on the truck's running board to reach the fuel cap, two men at the next pump looking over (seen from behind, faces not visible).`
- **Video:** `She hops down from the running board, landing with a little bounce, adjusts her cap, and gives a deadpan look to the camera. Camera from inside the truck through the open window.`

### 3. Top shelf problems
- **Hook:** "being short in a farm store is a sport"
- **Image:** `In a farm-supply store aisle, on tiptoes reaching for a bag on the top shelf, fingers just short of it.`
- **Video:** `She stretches on her tiptoes, jumps once trying to grab the bag, misses, then turns to the camera with an annoyed sigh. Friend filming from the aisle end.`

### 4. Out-fishing the boys
- **Hook:** "they said I couldn't fish 😂"
- **Image:** `On a small aluminum johnboat on a calm lake at sunrise, holding up a large largemouth bass with both hands, proud grin.`
- **Video:** `She lifts the bass toward the camera, laughs, and says something teasing to someone off camera. Gentle boat rocking, mist on the water.`

### 5. Washing Big Bertha (thirst trap, keep it clean)
- **Hook:** "y'all ask if I'm single. I'm busy."
- **Image:** `Washing the lifted black truck with a sponge, standing on a step stool to reach the hood, fitted tank top, denim shorts, soap suds, sunny day.`
- **Video:** `She scrubs the hood from the stool, wobbles, steadies herself, then flicks soap suds at the camera and laughs.`

### 6. The $30 fix (E-COM hero product)
- **Hook:** "the $30 thing that fixed my short girl problem"
- **Image:** `Standing on a small folding step that hooks onto the truck's door latch, reaching easily into the roof rack, satisfied smile.`
- **Video:** `She hooks the folding step onto the door latch, steps up onto it, reaches the roof rack easily and pats the roof, then looks at the camera and points down at the step.`

### 7. Moose rides shotgun
- **Hook:** "my passenger has opinions"
- **Image:** `In the driver seat, a chocolate labrador in the passenger seat, both looking out the windshield.`
- **Video:** `She turns to the dog and talks to him, the dog licks her face, she laughs and pushes him away. Camera mounted on the dashboard.`

### 8. Tailgate sunset
- **Hook:** "this is why I live out here"
- **Image:** `Sitting on the lowered tailgate in a cropped flannel and jeans, boots dangling high off the ground, field and orange sunset behind.`
- **Video:** `She swings her boots, takes a sip from a bottle, looks back at the camera with a soft smile. Slow handheld push-in.`

### 9. Parking lot hop-down
- **Hook:** "the dismount is everything"
- **Image:** `Sitting in the open driver door of the lifted truck in a parking lot, legs hanging, ground far below.`
- **Video:** `She turns, grabs the grab handle, and jumps down to the pavement, landing with a small stumble, then strikes a pose for the camera. Friend filming from the front.`

### 10. Bar line challenge
- **Hook:** "he thought I couldn't see over the bar"
- **Image:** `At a busy honky-tonk bar counter, only her head and shoulders showing over the bar, fitted black top, neon light.`
- **Video:** `She stands on the rail of the bar stool to lean over the counter, waves at the bartender, and grins back at the camera.`

## Troubleshooting
- Face drifts → reattach the closest reference image and add "identical face to reference."
- She looks too tall → add "the truck tire top reaches her waist, she is visibly much smaller than the truck."
- Weird motion → simplify the video prompt to ONE action.
- Change one thing per retry. Log your credits per usable video.
