# Dropshipping Plan: Kasey Mae's Gear

Same character, second funnel. Her height is the sales pitch: every product fixes a "short girl + big truck" problem or makes truck and outdoor life easier. Men buy it for their truck; couples buy it for the short girlfriend.

## Store
- **Name ideas:** "Little Lifted Co." / "Short Girl Truck Co." / "Bertha's Garage"
- **Platform:** Shopify (Basic) + DSers or AutoDS (suppliers: AliExpress, CJdropshipping)
- **Keep the store clean and separate:** no Fanvue mention anywhere on the store (payment processors drop shops tied to adult content).
- Disclose that Kasey is an AI character on the About page. **No fake reviews or fake "real customer" photos.**

## Bundle
"Short Girl Survival Kit": latch step + 2 grab handles, $49.99 (raises average order value).

## Product page template
- **Title:** "Truck Door Latch Step: Reach Your Roof Rack in Seconds"
- **Opening line:** "Built for short girls with big trucks (and the guys who are tired of lifting them)."
- 3 bullets: fits most trucks/SUVs with U-shaped door latches · folds to pocket size · holds up to [check supplier spec] lbs
- Video of Kasey using it (video #6 in PROMPTS.md)
- Shipping time stated honestly (e.g. "ships in 7–14 days")
- FAQ + returns policy

## Content-to-sale
- 1 in every 4 posts is a "gear fix" video. The other 3 build the audience.
- CTA: "comment 'STEP' and I'll send the link." Reply with the link-in-bio.
- Later: TikTok Shop listing (product tag right in the video), plus paid ads only once a product converts organically.

## Startup costs (approx)
- Shopify Basic: ~$39/mo (often $1 for the first months on promo)
- Domain: ~$15/yr
- DSers: free plan
- Test orders: buy 1 of each product yourself to check quality (~$30–50)
