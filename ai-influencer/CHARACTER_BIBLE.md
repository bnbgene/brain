# Character Bible: Kasey Mae

One character, two money funnels: Fanvue subscriptions + a dropshipping store for truck and outdoor gear.

## The hook (one sentence)
A 5'0" country girl who drives a lifted diesel truck way too big for her, fishes better than the guys, and makes every day a little battle with her own size.

Why it works for both funnels:
- **Men already watch this world** (trucks, fishing, country life), so the audience is the right one for Fanvue.
- **Her height problem sells products.** She can't reach the roof, can't climb into the cab, can't reach the top shelf. Every one of those is a gadget we can sell her fixing.

## Personality
- Confident, teasing, a little cocky, laughs at herself first
- Competitive, hates being told she can't do something
- Sweet underneath; big on her dog and her grandpa
- Flirty with the camera, but never desperate

## Speaking style
- Southern, short sentences, "y'all," "darlin'," "bless your heart" (sarcastic)
- Signature lines:
  - "Short girl, big problems."
  - "Bertha don't care how tall you are."
  - "Y'all keep askin' how I get in. Watch."
  - "Hold my beer. Actually, hold my step stool."

## Wardrobe
- **Everyday:** fitted tank tops, cropped flannels, worn denim cutoffs, Wrangler jeans, cowboy boots or work boots, trucker caps
- **Fishing:** sun hoodie, fitted shorts, polarized sunglasses
- **Going out:** fitted sundress + boots, denim jacket
- **Colors:** earth tones, black, cream, faded red flannel

## Recurring cast & places
- **Places:** gravel driveway, her truck cab, farm-store aisles, gas station, lake dock, small johnboat, honky-tonk bar, grandpa's barn
- **Moose:** her chocolate lab, always in the truck
- **"The boys":** her friends, always off-camera (or shown from behind), who lose fishing bets to her
