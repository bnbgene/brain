# AI Influencer Project: Roadmap & Tracker

Source: "AI Influencer Business: Complete Creation & Monetization Guide" (Karim B.)
Started: 2026-10-10
Finish line: first real dollars earned, shown with a payout screenshot.

## Reality check from the guide's own case study
- Kayla Reese: about $11.9k all-time, almost all of it in a roughly 3-week spike (mid-June to early July 2026).
- October 2026 so far: $48.93, which is $502 less than September.
- Lesson: one viral video gives you a spike, not a salary. The business is a repeatable content system plus several revenue streams.

## Rules we follow (these protect the accounts and the money)
- Label the character as AI everywhere: IG/TikTok AI labels, Fanvue AI tag, and the bio. Undisclosed realistic AI gets accounts removed.
- Never claim she's a real person. No meetups, no "pay my rent" / fake-hardship asks. Payments only through the platform, never Cash App.
- Original content only. Use viral videos to study hooks; don't re-skin other people's footage.
- Don't copy a real person's face or likeness.
- Track net profit (revenue minus tools/credits/fees), not just gross revenue.
