Why it matters in Phoenix

The desert is hard on concrete.

Between monsoon dust storms, hard water and 110° summers, Phoenix surfaces get dirty fast and stay stained. A proper wash keeps them looking sharp and helps them last.

  • Monsoon dustHaboobs coat everything in a layer of fine dirt.
  • Hard-water stainsSprinklers and pool splash leave white mineral rings.
  • Baked-in oil spotsThe summer heat sets oil drips deep into the driveway.
  • White haze on paversSalts rise out of block and pavers and leave a chalky film.
