# BNB Agent Farm

**Project:** [[BNB Profits]]
**Open it:** https://claude.ai/artifact/3fsux837tHKNAtZedkY33V
**Tip:** install it as its own app: Chrome ⋮ › Cast, save, and share › Install page as app (name it "BNB Farm").

## What it is
Your BNB Profits command center, built as a pixel-island game. Each AI "agent" has a desk on the island.
- **Content tab:** daily script to film, posting streak
- **Market Desk:** type your daily bias (before 6:45 AM) or hit "Not going live today"
- **Checklist tab:** strategy checklist (Eval / Funded modes), position-size calculator, daily loss guard
- **Trades tab:** log trades, or paste/import from Tradovate
- **Payouts tab:** prop account tracker (TPT Test/PRO rules), payout goal with a trophy and confetti
- **News Desk** and full-screen game mode

## What runs on its own
| Agent | When | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Script Writer | Daily 5:50 AM | Farm Content tab + your phone |
| News Desk | About every 2 hours | Your phone + #📰news (major only) |
| Market Desk (daily bias + chart) | Weekdays ~45 min before open | #🌅pre-market |
| Live in 10 | Weekdays, 10 min before open | #🌅pre-market |
| Recap Bot | Weekdays ~20 min after close | #📊recaps |
| Hype Man | Nightly ~6:52 PM | #💬general |
| Payout Hype | When you log a payout | #💰payouts |
| Week Ahead | Sundays ~4:52 PM | #📅weekly-outlook |
| Lead alerts | When someone applies on the site | #applicants |

## Your daily part (~5 min)
- Morning: film the Content tab script; optionally set your bias in Market Desk.
- After close: log your trades.
- When paid: log the payout.
