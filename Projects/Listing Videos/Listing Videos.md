## Goal
Help Airbnb hosts get more bookings. Most listings only have photos. We turn their photos into a smooth video walkthrough, and build them a direct-booking website with the video on top.

## The offer
| Package | What they get | Price idea |
|---|---|---|
| **Video** | 45-sec walkthrough (wide for website/YouTube + vertical for Reels/TikTok) | $99–$199 |
| **Video + Site** | Video + 1-page booking website with the video as the header | $499–$999 setup, then $29–$49/mo hosting |
| **Monthly** | New seasonal video + 4 social clips a month | $99/mo |

## How it works (no visit needed)
1. Grab the photos from their public listing.
2. Put them in walkthrough order: front of house → door → living → kitchen → bedrooms → baths → backyard → night shot.
3. Slow zoom/pan on each photo, smooth fades, room labels, end card with rating + "Book your stay".
4. Add music (royalty-free) and send.

## Where the video goes
- ⚠️ Airbnb may not let hosts upload video to the listing itself. Check the listing editor before promising "on your Airbnb page."
- Sure places: their own booking site, Instagram/TikTok/Facebook, Google Business Profile, YouTube. VRBO and Booking.com are worth checking too.
- Selling point for the site: guests booking direct skip Airbnb's ~14% guest fee, and the host keeps the guest's contact info.

## How to find hosts
- Search Airbnb in Phoenix/Scottsdale for homes with 4.8★+ and no video. Message hosts through Facebook groups for STR owners, or find them on Instagram.
- Make the video FIRST, then DM it: "I made this from your listing photos. Want it?" The free sample is the pitch.
- Property managers with 10+ homes = bigger deals.
