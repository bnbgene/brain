# Competitor: SmartWiz (smartwiz.ai, formerly smartwiz.io)

**Project:** [[W-2 Helper]] · Researched 2026-11-12

## What they do
- AI assistant "Wizzy" reads W-2s, 1099s, K-1s and intake forms (PDF/photo), builds a client profile, then **One-Click Return** enters it into the tax software (browser extension). Preparer reviews and files.
- Much bigger scope than us: whole 1040 (Schedule C, credits, state), client intake links, Jotform/Cognito/GoHighLevel imports, review queue, due diligence tools.
- Software: TaxSlayer **Pro Web** (not desktop), Drake Online (+ Drake Desktop early access), CrossLink **Online**, OLT Pro; TaxWise mentioned on an old page. **No sign of E Tax (cloudtaxoffice).**
- Security: SOC 2 **Type I**, encrypted, "never trains AI on your data". Client data is uploaded to *their* cloud.

## Pricing
- $18–20/return (50–199 returns), $14–16/return (200–2,499), custom above. 20% deposit up front, 50-return minimum.
- Bank-product package: 50 returns for $1,250 ($25/return).
- Through Santa Barbara TPG (bank partner): $9/return, 11 returns for $99 to start.

## Where we win
- [ ] **E Tax offices**: SmartWiz doesn't support E Tax. We already work on it.
- [ ] **Price**: our AI cost is ~5–10¢ per W-2. Flat, cheap season price (e.g. $149–299/office) vs their $9–20 per return + deposit.
- [ ] **Privacy**: nothing stored, runs on the office PC, office's own AI account. "We never hold your clients' data."
- [ ] **Small offices**: no minimums, no deposit, personal setup.

## Where they win (don't fight here yet)
- Full returns, all forms, intake, bank partnerships, SOC 2, many software brands.
