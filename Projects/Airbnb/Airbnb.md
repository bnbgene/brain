## The idea
Most Airbnb listings only have photos. Turn a host's photos into a smooth **video walkthrough**, and build them a **direct-booking website** with the video on top.

## The offer
| Package | What they get | Price idea |
|---|---|---|
| **Video** | 45-sec walkthrough (wide + vertical for Reels/TikTok) | $99–$199 |
| **Video + Site** | Video + 1-page booking site with the video as the header | $499–$999 setup, then $29–$49/mo |
| **Monthly** | New seasonal video + 4 social clips a month | $99/mo |

## How it works (no visit needed)
1. Grab the photos from their public listing.
2. Put them in walkthrough order: front → door → living → kitchen → bedrooms → baths → backyard → night shot.
3. AI depth on each photo so the camera walks into each room (slight walking sway, push through to the next room), room labels, end card with rating + "Book your stay".
4. Add royalty-free music and send.

## Where the video goes
- ⚠️ Airbnb may not allow host video on the listing itself. Check before promising it.
- Sure places: their own booking site, Instagram/TikTok/Facebook, Google Business Profile, YouTube. Check VRBO and Booking.com too.
- Site selling point: direct bookings skip Airbnb's ~14% guest fee, and the host keeps the guest's contact info.

## How to find hosts
- Search Airbnb in Phoenix/Scottsdale for 4.8★+ homes with no video. Reach hosts in STR-owner Facebook groups or on Instagram.
- Make the video FIRST, then DM it: "I made this from your listing photos. Want it?"
- Property managers with 10+ homes = bigger deals.
