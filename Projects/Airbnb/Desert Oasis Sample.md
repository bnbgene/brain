# Desert Oasis Sample

**Project:** [[Airbnb]]
**Open the sample booking site:** https://claude.ai/artifact/ARwRs8Gqzfz1PHCboFvVUH (marked as a mockup)

## What it is
First example of the service, made from a real listing: "Desert Oasis" in Scottsdale (4 bed, sleeps 10, 4.98★, 58 photos, no video).
- 44-second walkthrough video: 16 photos with slow zoom/pan, room captions, and a "Book your stay" end card
- Sample booking website with the video as the header

## Video maker
[tools/listing-video/make.py](https://github.com/bnbgene/brain/blob/claude/charming-cori-om30sj/tools/listing-video/make.py): turns listing photos into the walkthrough video. Reusable for the next host.
