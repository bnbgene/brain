NQ · 1m · NY openExample

  1. Mark the levels

    Previous session highs and lows. That's where the liquidity sits, meaning everyone's stops.

  2. Wait for the sweep

    Let price run one side and take those stops. No sweep, no trade.

  3. Get confirmation

    Drop to the 1-minute. I want a fair value gap or a break of structure before I'm in.

  4. Target the other side

    Opposing liquidity is the target. Risk is set before I click anything.

Then walk away. Take what the market gives you.
