# Brain Farm

**Open it:** https://claude.ai/artifact/CPX3KFsqmPATL2M2yLBhwd

## What it does
- Shows every project as a plot on a pixel farm, with each Claude chat as a farmer.
- Crops are tall and green when a project was worked on recently. They wilt and grow weeds after 2 weeks with no work.
- Tap a plot to see its last result, its next step, and what to say to get it moving again.
- "Empty plots" lists farmhands you can add, like scheduled workers.
